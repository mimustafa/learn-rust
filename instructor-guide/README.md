# Instructor guide

Student-facing lessons are in [`../days`](../days). Keep learners on the student pages until they have attempted that day's work.

## Teaching cadence

Each 60-minute session can use: 10 minutes reading/objective check, 15 minutes live typing, 20 minutes exercise, 10 minutes review/compiler discussion, and 5 minutes architecture/recap. Days with a database or async lab may trade lecture for guided practice, but do not add hours of homework disguised as a one-hour lesson.
